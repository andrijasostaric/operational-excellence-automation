# Control Framework

| Control ID | Control objective | Control activity | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VC-01 | Only complete requests enter review | Mandatory fields validated before submission | Operations | Intake record |
| VC-02 | Risk determines review depth | Risk tier assigned using agreed criteria | Risk / Operations | Risk classification |
| VC-03 | Required specialists approve relevant risks | Conditional routing to Compliance / Security / Legal | Control owner | Approval record |
| VC-04 | Vendor setup occurs only after approval | Workflow blocks setup until approvals complete | Finance / Operations | Workflow history |
| VC-05 | Decisions remain traceable | Approver, timestamp and outcome recorded | Operations | Approval log |
| VC-06 | Evidence is retained centrally | Supporting documents linked to vendor record | Vendor owner | Evidence index |
| VC-07 | Vendors are periodically reassessed | Review date generated by risk tier | Vendor owner | Review schedule |

## Control philosophy

The aim is not to automate judgement. Automation should enforce sequence, completeness and traceability while accountable people retain responsibility for material decisions and risk acceptance.
