# Operational Excellence & Automation

A fictional financial-services case study showing how a manual vendor-onboarding process can be redesigned, controlled and partially automated.

This repository is a **synthetic portfolio example**. It contains no employer, customer, vendor or confidential information.

## Scenario

A fictional financial-services company uses email and spreadsheets to onboard new vendors. Requests arrive inconsistently, approvals are hard to trace, evidence is scattered, and stakeholders have limited visibility of status.

## Objective

Create a controlled workflow that:
- standardises intake and required information;
- clarifies ownership and approval points;
- embeds risk and compliance checks;
- reduces manual follow-up;
- improves auditability and reporting;
- gives stakeholders a clear status view.

## Delivery approach

The case study uses process discovery, RACI-style ownership, control design, Power Automate-style workflow logic, exception handling and KPI monitoring. The emphasis is on business value and controlled delivery rather than coding.
